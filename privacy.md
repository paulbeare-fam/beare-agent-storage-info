# Beare Agent Storage privacy policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026.

Beare Agent Storage is operated by Paul Beare for private household use. It uses Google Drive to store project documents and encrypted recovery backups.

## Google account information and access

Google account information is used to confirm that the intended account has authorised the connection. The application requests the **drive.file** permission to manage application-created or explicitly authorised files. Depending on the enabled workflow, it creates, reads, updates, archives and restores those files.

OAuth credentials are retained on the household server in restricted files so that the storage process can continue without repeated sign-in. Credentials are not provided to language-model agents.

## Storage and use

Recovery backup bundles are encrypted before upload to Drive. Ordinary project documents are not separately encrypted by this application. Files are stored in the authorised Google Drive account; operational files and recovery copies are also retained on household infrastructure.

The application does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising. The storage connection does not itself send Google credentials or Drive content to an AI service. Separately enabled agent workflows may send owner-approved task inputs to their configured AI service.

## Retention and control

Recovery points are retained until the owner reviews a retention policy. This release does not automatically prune them.

Access can be revoked through the Google Account's third-party connections settings. Revocation does not itself remove files already stored in Drive. The account owner can manage those files in Drive and arrange removal of retained local copies with the operator.

## Contact

For questions about this application or its stored data, contact [Paul Beare through his GitHub profile](https://github.com/paulbeare-fam).
